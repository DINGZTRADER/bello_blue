# Bello & Blue Events — 90-Day Growth Plan

## Primary commercial objective
Turn the website into a steady source of qualified event enquiries, not just an online brochure.

## Month 1 — Trust and discoverability
- Connect the final Bello & Blue domain to the existing Vercel project.
- Enable Vercel Web Analytics.
- Create or fully optimize the Google Business Profile.
- Add the website URL to Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Business and all digital flyers.
- Keep the same phone number and brand name across every platform.
- Add 6–10 strong real event photographs as the portfolio grows.

## Month 2 — Enquiry generation
- Publish recent-event posts that link directly to the quotation section.
- Use two campaign angles: `Plan My Event` and `Hire Event Equipment`.
- Add customer reviews/testimonials only when they are real and approved.
- Track which enquiry source produces the strongest leads.
- Prioritize weddings, corporate launches and equipment hire because they can generate higher-value enquiries.

## Month 3 — Conversion optimization
- Review analytics for WhatsApp clicks, call clicks and quote submissions.
- Identify which service pages/sections attract the most engagement.
- Add pricing guidance only if Bello & Blue wants to pre-qualify budgets; do not publish arbitrary fixed prices for custom events.
- Expand the equipment catalogue with real inventory photos and quantities.
- Add a dedicated gallery/case-study section once enough genuine events are available.

## Recommended monthly metrics
Track:
- website visitors
- WhatsApp clicks
- telephone clicks
- structured quote submissions
- qualified enquiries
- quotations sent
- confirmed bookings
- average booking value
- enquiry-to-booking conversion rate

## Commercial rule
Every future website feature should improve at least one of these outcomes: more qualified enquiries, faster quoting, higher booking value, stronger trust, or less administrative work.
