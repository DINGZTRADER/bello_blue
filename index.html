Can I hire equipment without full event planning?

Yes. Bello & Blue offers event equipment hire separately. Choose “Equipment hire only” and select the items you are interested in.

Is delivery and setup available?

Yes. Delivery and setup are available. Include your event location so the team can respond appropriately.

How do I check whether my date and equipment are available?

Send your event date and hire list through the quotation form. Availability depends on the date and items required.

What kinds of events do you handle?

Weddings, corporate events, commercial launches, graduations, kids parties, baby showers, bridal showers, press conferences and other celebrations.

Why is there no fixed price list?

Requirements vary by date, location, guest count, equipment and level of planning support. The website collects those details so Bello & Blue can prepare a relevant quotation.
